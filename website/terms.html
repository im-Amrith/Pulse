6Changes Pulse makes on your computer

When you choose Connect, Pulse adds its own entries to your Claude Code settings file (~/.claude/settings.json) and keeps a backup next to it (settings.json.pulse-backup). Disconnect removes only Pulse's entries. If Pulse is moved to another folder, it updates its own entries the next time it starts. Pulse can also open when you log in if you turn that on, watch your Downloads folder if that setting is on, and change your system volume when you use its volume control. On macOS it may ask for permission to control Spotify or Music and to record the screen (for "Look at my screen"); you can refuse or withdraw these at any time in System Settings. You are responsible for checking that these changes suit your setup.

7Privacy

Pulse runs on your computer and has no account system. It does not send your data to the author. Some features contact other services, only when you use them:

  • Ask Pulse sends your question (and, if you choose "Look at my screen", a screenshot) to Claude through your own Claude Code installation and account.
  • Lyrics look up the title, artist and length of the song that is playing on lrclib.net. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Update checks ask GitHub for the latest Pulse version number.
  • Phone transfer serves files over your local network to devices you connect with its QR code.

Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, which apply to your use of them.
